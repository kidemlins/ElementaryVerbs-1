The dative marks the person on the receiving end — of a gift, a message, a favour. It also carries two frames you'll use constantly: sviđa mi se (I like it) and treba mi (I need it).

Watch outBefore the feminine -i, a final k becomes c and a final g becomes z: majka → majci, knjiga → knjizi. This trips up almost everyone at A1.

The dative and the locative look the same in the singular. If there's no preposition, or the preposition is k or prema, it's dative.
