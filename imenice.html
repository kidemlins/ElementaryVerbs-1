plurals.

Every Croatian noun carries a gender, and the gender decides every ending that follows. Nine times out of ten the last letter tells you: -a feminine, -o / -e neuter, a consonant masculine.

-aženski (ž)-ekuća → kuće
-o / -esrednji (s)-aselo → sela
-ost, -avženski (ž)-istvar → stvari
The exceptions worth knowingShort masculine nouns usually add -ovi or -evi in the plural: stol → stolovi, ključ → ključevi. And a handful of masculine nouns end in -a and behave like feminines: tata, kolega, sudac.

Words that only exist in the plural: vrata (door), leđa (back), novine (newspaper), naočale (glasses).
