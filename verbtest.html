Naming a verb through English puts a station in the middle of the line: picture → write → pisati. Here that station is removed. Concrete verbs come as a drawing; abstract ones come as two Croatian sentences with the verb missing. Say the answer out loud before you reveal it.

ⰀWhy some verbs aren't drawn

A picture can show an action, but it can't show a distinction. Two verbs that look identical on paper are separated by the sentence they live in.

  • sjediti / sjesti
  • ležati / leći
  • kupiti / kupovati
  • čuti / slušati
  • tražiti / naći
  • moći / smjeti
  • živjeti / stanovati
  • ići / hodati
The pair to learn firstPosition takes the locative, movement takes the accusative. Sjedim na stolici — I'm sitting on the chair. Sjednem na stolicu — I sit down on the chair. The same split runs through ležati / leći and stajati / stati.

The six slots are always in this order: ja · ti · on/ona · mi · vi · oni. A dash means the verb only works in the third person — boli me glava, not bolim.
