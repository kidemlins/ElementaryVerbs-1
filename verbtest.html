Knowing a Croatian verb means knowing two things: how it conjugates, and which case it drags along behind it. Pomoći takes the dative, bojati se takes the genitive — get the pairing wrong and the sentence collapses.

The six slots are always in this order: ja · ti · on/ona · mi · vi · oni.
