Where,
and where to.

kamo? ↔ gdje? · 20 questions per round

One preposition, two cases, two meanings. u + accusative means you're heading there; u + locative means you're already there. Get this pair straight and a large slice of everyday Croatian falls into place.

ⰀThe pair, side by side

Where to — accusativeWhere — locative
gradIdem u grad.Ja sam u gradu.
školaIdem u školu.Ja sam u školi.
posaoIdem na posao.Ja sam na poslu.
moreIdem na more.Ja sam na moru.
plažaIdem na plažu.Ja sam na plaži.
Two things to watchBefore the feminine locative -i, k becomes c and g becomes z: banka → u banci, knjiga → o knjizi. And masculine animate nouns take -a in the accusative: Čekam prijatelja, but Čekam autobus.

Ⰱu or na?

There's no rule you can derive — these come in pairs and are simply learned.

  • u grad
  • u školu
  • u kino
  • u trgovinu
  • u restoran

  • na posao
  • na more
  • na plažu
  • na otok
  • na tržnicu
  • na poštu
  • na fakultet
  • na koncert
  • na planinu

The locative never appears without a preposition in front of it — u, na, o, po or pri.
