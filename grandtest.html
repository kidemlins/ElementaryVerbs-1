The place triadAlmost every place follows the same three-step pattern: motion in (accusative), sitting still (locative), coming back out (genitive). u trgovinu → u trgovini → iz trgovine. Places that take na instead of u swap the last step for s, not iz: na posao → na poslu → s posla.

ⰁThe thing stays put

Two of the most common everyday frames put the person in the dative but leave the thing in the nominative — a frequent source of mistakes.

  • Sviđa mi se kava.
  • Treba mi vrijeme.
  • Nedostaje mi obitelj.
Watch outkava, vrijeme and obitelj above are not accusative objects — they're the grammatical subject. Only the person doing the liking, needing or missing moves into the dative.
