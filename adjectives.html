A Croatian adjective copies the gender, number and case of its noun. dobar film, dobra kava, dobro vino — one word, three shapes, and that's before the cases get involved.

The vanishing a, againAdjectives ending in -an or -ar lose that a as soon as an ending is added: dobar → dobra, umoran → umorna, ružan → ružna, topao → topla. Only the bare masculine keeps it.

Two intensifiers worth having on hand: vrlo and jako both mean “very”, and malo means “a bit”.
