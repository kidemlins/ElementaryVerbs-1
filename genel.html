Croatian nouns change shape depending on the job they do in the sentence. There are seven shapes, called padež. Tap a square below to see when that case is used, the endings for each gender, the pronouns, and sentences you'd actually say.

ⰊPersonal pronouns

ⰌQuestion frames

The fastest way into a case: learn the question it answers, then copy the answer shape.

QuestionMeansCaseAnswer
Tko je to?Who is that?NTo je Ana.
Što je to?What is that?NTo je kava.
Čega nema?What's missing?GNema mlijeka.
Odakle si?Where are you from?G iz + GIz Turske sam.
Koliko košta?How much is it?G 5+ → gen. pl.Pet eura.
Komu daješ?Who are you giving it to?DPrijatelju.
Što želiš?What do you want?AŽelim pivo.
Kamo ideš?Where are you going?A u/na + AIdem u grad.
Gdje si?Where are you?L u/na + LU kafiću sam.
O čemu pričaš?What are you talking about?LO poslu.
S kim ideš?Who are you going with?IS bratom.
Čime ideš?What are you travelling by?IAutobusom.

Suggested order: accusative → locative → genitive → instrumental → dative → vocative. Roughly 80% of everyday speech runs on the first three.
