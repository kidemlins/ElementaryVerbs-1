The busiest case after the accusative. It covers possession, absence, quantities, dates, and the long list of prepositions that govern it. Learn nema + genitive first and a whole family of sentences comes free.

The vanishing aSeveral masculine nouns drop the a in their last syllable once an ending is added: posao → posla, ručak → ručka, centar → centra, novac → novca, petak → petka. It catches everyone out at first.

Counting rule: 2, 3 and 4 take the genitive singular; 5 and above take the genitive plural. dvije kave but pet kava.
