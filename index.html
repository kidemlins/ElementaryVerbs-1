ⰊWhere to start

You don't need all seven cases to talk. This order gets you speaking fastest.

OrderCaseBuys you
1AccusativeIdem u grad.Ordering, wanting, going somewhere
2LocativeU kafiću sam.Saying where you are
3GenitiveNemam vremena.Not having, quantities, most prepositions
4InstrumentalIdem autobusom.With whom, by what
5DativeSviđa mi se.Liking, needing, giving to someone
6VocativeOprostite, gospodine!Getting someone's attention
One rule to carryMotion takes the accusative, standing still takes the locative. Idem u grad — I'm going into town. Ja sam u gradu — I'm in town. Same preposition, two cases, two meanings.
